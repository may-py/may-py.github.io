FAQ

Common questions

Can you help a foreign company set up in Vietnam?

Yes. We support the full incorporation process — statutory compliance, government approvals, company formation, bank account opening and legal agreements.

Can I use HRSM only for bookkeeping and tax?

Absolutely. Many clients come to us for ongoing accounting, payroll, income tax and VAT/GST compliance alone.

Which countries do you cover?

We work directly with clients in Vietnam and India. For the Philippines, we refer you to a trusted partner firm.

Can you help us find partners or distributors?

Yes. Our pre-incorporation services include market studies, finding business partners and distributors, and representation at business forums and associations.
